About Quantum Water

In Quantum Water you run a desalination plant for a day. You pick 3 of 5 time slots for the lowest electricity bill, then press Simulate and watch a Grover quantum search look for a cheaper plan. The maths follows the desalination notebook from the KACST Qiskit Fall Fest 2026. Everything quantum is simulated, and the game says so when a plain loop would be faster.

What is Quantum Water?

Quantum Water is a free 3D browser game. You run a desalination plant, pick 3 of 5 time slots for the lowest electricity bill, then watch a simulated quantum search look for a cheaper plan. Everything quantum in the game is simulated.

Who are the characters in Quantum Water?

You meet six helpers in the plant: Fay (guide), Bushra (prices), Lina (restarts), Mohammed (engineer), Hassan (planner) and James (quantum).
