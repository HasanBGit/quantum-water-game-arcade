# Playtest sheet: can a student follow the game?

Needs: 1 middle-school student (11-14) and 1 high-school student (15-18), one at a time. About 15 minutes each. Use English or Arabic (`?lang=ar`).

## How to run it
1. Say only: "Play this game. Think out loud. I will not help." Do not explain anything.
2. Watch, and write down where they stop, re-read, or look confused.
3. After the game, ask the questions below. Do not hint. Write what they say.
4. They may use the **Words** button during the game. Note which words they opened.

## Questions and what a right answer looks like
| # | Ask | Right answer (from the game) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What was your job? | Pick 3 of 5 time slots to run the pumps for the lowest bill. |
| 2 | What is a cold start / restart? | A pump starting after being off costs extra; a pump that keeps running does not pay again. |
| 3 | Is the 3 cheapest slots always the best plan? Why not? | No: spread-out slots pay several restarts. |
| 4 | What is "the bar"? | The bill to beat; it moves down when a cheaper plan is found. |
| 5 | How does the quantum search differ from the clerk? | Clerk checks one by one; the search raises the chance of cheaper plans, then measures. |
| 6 | Does measuring give all good plans or one? Why repeat? | One, picked by chance; so repeat. |
| 7 | Was the quantum search faster here? | No: it is only a simulation, a plain loop is faster for 32 plans. |
| 8 | Which words were confusing? Which Words entries helped? | (free answer) |

## Scoring
- Pass: the student gets Q1, Q2, Q3 and Q7 right without help, and at least 5 of 7 overall.
- Also record: time to finish, where they got stuck, any word they could not explain.
- Send the notes back and the text can be fixed where students got lost.
